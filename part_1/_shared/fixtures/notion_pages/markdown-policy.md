# Markdown policy

Kata protects the full-price position of technical basics. Markdown is a release valve
for seasonal risk, not the default response to a slow week. Evaluate sell-through,
remaining full-price weeks, regional stock cover, gross margin and transfer options in
that order.

For seasonal lines, prepare a 15% markdown when sell-through is at least ten points
behind plan with fewer than eight trading weeks left. Increase to 25% with fewer than
five weeks left, or 35% inside the final two weeks. A deeper cut needs trading-director
approval. Core products do not enter routine seasonal markdown: fix availability,
assortment or placement first. Never recommend a markdown solely because revenue is
below plan when units are healthy; price and discount mix may explain the gap.

Margin guardrail: forecast the gross-margin value after the proposed discount and show
the break-even unit uplift. Regional profitability must use net revenue less unit cost,
not revenue rank. EU volume has recently been supported by broad promotion; review its
gross-margin value and percent before extending any event.

Exclude products with an agreed hold from the recommendation queue. AirLight currently
has a recorded decision to hold markdown until after the Japan drop. ERPA may report
the decision and its expiry, but must not propose the action again.

Every recommendation records SKU, region, current ST%, plan ST%, remaining weeks,
current and proposed price, forecast unit uplift, margin impact, and decision owner.
Markdowns require human approval and are never written to a commerce system by ERPA.
