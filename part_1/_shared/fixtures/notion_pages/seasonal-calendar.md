# Seasonal trading calendar — AW26 / SS27

AW26 Outerwear launches first in Japan on 3 September, then UK/EU on 10 September and
US on 17 September. The first ThermaCore campaign window runs 28 September–18 October.
WarmLayer receives continuous core intake, but its cold-market presentation moves to
front-of-store when the seven-day mean temperature falls below 12°C.

Japan's autumn holiday drop is 22 September. The AirLight Japan hold remains in place
until the post-drop read on 30 September; do not recommend a markdown before that
review. Southeast Asia keeps AirLight in continuous summer presentation and does not
follow the northern-hemisphere thermal calendar.

Golden Week planning starts 12 February. UK/EU winter sale preview begins 18 December,
with public launch 26 December. The US holiday event starts 24 November. Regional
markdown dates are not interchangeable: use the line's regional exit date when testing
remaining full-price weeks.

Weekly merchandising review is Thursday at 14:00 Europe/London. Pre-read is due by
12:00 Wednesday. Vendor calls must respect the planner preference of no meeting before
10:00 local time. The calendar integration has read and create scopes only; it may not
delete or move an existing meeting.

Weather can change presentation and short-term demand but does not automatically
change the seasonal exit date. Use an external signal to explain an anomalous spike,
then keep internal transaction data as the evidence that the spike occurred.
