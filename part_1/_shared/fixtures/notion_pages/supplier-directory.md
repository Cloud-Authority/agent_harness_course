# Supplier directory and terms

**Hikari Technical Textiles (`SUP-01`)** supplies ThermaCore and specialist insulated
styles. Standard lead time is 35 days, reliability 0.96, minimum 500 units. Expedited
air freight is possible only for hero sizes and requires Sourcing approval.

**NordWeave Manufacturing (`SUP-02`)** makes merino-blend DailyKnit and selected
Outerwear. Lead time is 28 days, reliability 0.91, minimum 350 units. Colour minimums
apply; natural shades can be combined within one order.

**Pearl River Basics (`SUP-03`)** produces high-volume core Innerwear. Lead time is 42
days, reliability 0.88, minimum 750 units. Add a seven-day risk buffer when calculating
projected cover. Escalate shortages because reliability is below the 0.90 threshold.

**Izmir Cotton Works (`SUP-04`)** supplies PureCotton. Lead time is 24 days,
reliability 0.94, minimum 400 units. Repeat core colours may use reserved greige fabric,
which reduces confirmed lead time to 18 days.

**Da Nang Performance (`SUP-05`)** supplies AirLight, FieldShell and selected bags.
Lead time is 31 days, reliability 0.92, minimum 450 units. Seasonal cancellations are
accepted until cutting with a 5% fee.

Lead time begins when the supplier acknowledges a purchase order, not when a planner
drafts it. ERPA can calculate and recommend quantities but cannot raise or amend an
order. Always include the supplier ID, minimum, reliability and expected receipt date
in restock recommendations. Vendor comments are internal and must not be included in
customer-facing output.
