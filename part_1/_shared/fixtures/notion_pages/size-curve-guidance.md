# Size-curve guidance

Size curve is evaluated within product and colour, never by category total. The global
starting curve for apparel is XXS 4%, XS 10%, S 20%, M 25%, L 22%, XL 13%, XXL 6%.
Use it only when the regional override below is unavailable.

UK and EU demand is centred on M/L: 3%, 8%, 19%, 27%, 25%, 13%, 5%. Japan shifts one
step smaller: 7%, 16%, 25%, 26%, 17%, 7%, 2%. US demand has a larger XL/XXL tail: 2%,
6%, 15%, 23%, 25%, 18%, 11%. Southeast Asia resembles Japan for Innerwear but uses the
global curve for unisex Outerwear.

Flag a broken curve when a size's actual demand share differs from target by more than
eight percentage points, or a hero size has under one week of cover while total product
cover is above four. Do not solve a curve failure with a product-wide reorder. Recommend
the size quantities needed to restore the regional curve, subject to case packs and
minimum order quantities.

For the ThermaCore hero jacket, M and L are the commercial centre. Current stock is
concentrated in XS and XXL while M/L are near sell-out. Treat this as a size-curve
failure even where aggregate cover looks comfortable. Show stock by region and size so
the imbalance is visible.

Return reason `size` is an additional diagnostic. A high size-return rate may mean the
garment fit is wrong rather than the buy curve. TrueDenim is under fit review; do not
automatically shift its curve until Product confirms the block.
