# Regional merchandising notes

UK customers respond quickly to temperature changes. WarmLayer is usually merchandised
with Outerwear once the seven-day mean falls below 12°C; cold snaps can move demand
before the campaign team changes media. London stores favour black, navy and middle
sizes. Report UK values in GBP and use Monday–Sunday complete weeks.

EU trading is split across climates, so do not treat Berlin and Paris inventory as
interchangeable without a transfer check. Germany over-indexes in technical Outerwear;
France has a stronger DailyKnit mix. Recent EU revenue is healthy but broad discounting
has compressed gross margin. Profitability answers must show margin value and rate,
not revenue alone.

Japan launches seasonal technical lines earlier and skews smaller in size. AirLight
remains relevant into early autumn, particularly in southern prefectures. Use the
Japan-specific size curve and honour the current post-drop markdown hold.

US stores carry deeper XL/XXL quantities and have a higher average unit retail. Returns
should be separated into fit and non-fit reasons. Southeast Asia is effectively
year-round summer for Innerwear; ThermaCore demand there is travel-led and should not
inherit northern winter forecasts.

Alex Moreau owns UK and EU planning for Outerwear and Tops. Morning attention lists
must stay inside that scope unless Alex explicitly requests a global comparison.
Accessories are excluded from Alex's recurring output. A one-off direct question about
Accessories is still answerable, but must not silently change the saved exclusion.
